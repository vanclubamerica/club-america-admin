# 6. Leadership transfer

The yearly handover. Follow this in order — it is designed so nothing is lost and nobody gets locked out.

**Who does this:** the outgoing President (the account Owner), ideally with the Teacher Sponsor present.

**When:** at the end of the school year, once new officers are elected.

---

## Before you start

Open **Leadership Transfer** in the dashboard. Everything below happens on that page.

Have ready: the incoming officers' **names and email addresses**.

---

## Step 1 — Print the handoff report

On the Leadership Transfer page, find **Leadership handoff report** and press **Print**.

It lists the current website status, every officer, all admin accounts, active sponsors, documents on file, and the meeting details. It also flags gaps — officers with no photo, for example.

**Print two copies.** One for the outgoing president's records, one to hand to the new president. Walking through it together is the single most useful part of this whole process.

---

## Step 2 — Invite the new officers

Under **Invite an incoming officer**, add each new officer with their name, email, and role.

They receive an email and **set their own password**. You never see it, and neither does anyone else — that is why there are no shared logins.

Have each new officer sign in once to confirm it works, *before* you continue.

---

## Step 3 — Update the website

The new team should now update the public site with their own information:

- **Officers** — new names, photos, and biographies for all five main roles, plus any additional positions
- **About** — refresh anything that names the outgoing team or last year's activities
- **Sponsors** — remove any that have lapsed
- **Settings** — update meeting day, time, and location if they have changed

Then **publish**. The new team's names are now live.

---

## Step 4 — Transfer ownership

Under **Transfer ownership**, choose the new President and type `TRANSFER` to confirm.

This hands over control of accounts, the emergency lock, and this page. **You keep full content permissions** — all five roles can always edit and publish. Only the administrative controls move.

> There is always exactly one owner. The database enforces this, so the club can never end up with zero owners or two.

---

## Step 5 — Archive the school year

Under **Archive this school year**, confirm the year (e.g. `2026-2027`) and add any notes worth passing on — things that went well, things to do differently, sponsors to follow up with.

This freezes the roster and the handoff report into permanent history. **Archived years can never be deleted**, so future officers will always be able to see who served when.

---

## Step 6 — Suspend the outgoing accounts

Under **Account status**, set each departing officer to **Suspended**.

Do this **last**, after the new team has verified they can sign in and publish. Suspending too early can leave nobody able to make changes.

- **Suspended** — cannot sign in; can be reactivated
- **Archived** — permanently retired

Their name stays in the activity logs and the archived roster either way. Suspending an account never erases their history.

---

## About the Teacher Sponsor account

The Teacher Sponsor is marked as a **recovery account**. It keeps the ability to manage accounts and lift an emergency lock even after ownership moves to a new student president.

This exists because students graduate and passwords get lost. Keep at least one adult with this flag at all times — it is the club's way back in when something goes wrong over the summer.

---

## Handover checklist

Give this to the outgoing president.

- [ ] Handoff report printed, two copies
- [ ] All incoming officers invited
- [ ] Every new officer has signed in successfully
- [ ] Officers page updated with new names, photos, and bios
- [ ] About page updated
- [ ] Lapsed sponsors removed
- [ ] Meeting details confirmed in Settings
- [ ] Changes published and checked on tpvan.com
- [ ] Ownership transferred to the new President
- [ ] School year archived, with notes
- [ ] Outgoing accounts suspended
- [ ] Teacher Sponsor still has recovery access
- [ ] New president knows how to reach Brant Borden for technical problems
